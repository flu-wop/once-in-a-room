The idea

Most design fills a room.
We design the one thing in it worth noticing.

Once In A Room is an interior design consultancy built around a simple belief: a room doesn't need more in it, it needs one considered decision done right — a lamp, a layout, a material — that gives the whole space a reason to be remembered.

Consultation-first

We start with how you live in the space, not a mood board.

Sourced, not stocked

Every recommendation is sourced for your room specifically — nothing off a shelf.

Built to last

Plans that hold up in five years, not five months of trend cycles.
